# What each reader was told besides the brief

Every reader got `reader-brief.md` verbatim, its packet paths, where to write
its answers, and these common rules: judge every block exactly once; every
verdict is the reader's own reading, never a program or keyword rule (Python
only to list blocks, write the JSON and check coverage); use only the packets
and the brief; modify nothing in the repository; report verdict counts and
every non-agreeing or covered-elsewhere block.

The wrappers were not identical. What each added is recorded here, because two
of them carried the executor's knowledge into the reading, and a verdict on a
block of that kind is therefore not independent of it.

| Reader | Packets | Added to the brief |
|---|---|---|
| Enphase | FY2021-FY2024 | Restated the brief's distinctions: page furniture is never disclosure; a context paragraph whose lawsuit a taken block describes is covered elsewhere; leases, purchase obligations, tariffs, warranties and tables under a contingencies note are correctly skipped unless they describe a proceeding or claim. |
| Ford | FY2021-FY2024 | This company's Item 3 incorporates a whole note, so its substantive content is disclosure even where it concerns guarantees or other commitments; the company's running heads listed as furniture. |
| Lumen | FY2021-FY2024 | Item 3 incorporates named subheadings of a note; an ITEM_8 excerpt needs its own content to be a disclosure of the registrant's legal matters - **"a fee policy that lists 'litigation' among matters counsel advises on, for example, is not"**. That example is the latest-year Lumen defect (block 1670). A verdict on such a block in these packets is not independent of that finding. |
| Macy's, Marriott | Macy's FY2021-FY2024, Marriott FY2021-FY2022 | The meaning of ITEM_8 and NOTE_ scopes; **"a block that is only numbers (a table's column years) states no matter by itself"** - the executor's adjudication on Ford's header rows, given before this reader started; and that covered-elsewhere needs the cited blocks to state the matter. |
| Paramount, Pfizer, Salesforce, Southwest | the rest | The meaning of ITEM_8 and NOTE_ scopes only, with no example drawn from any earlier finding. |
